1. What LiveShows does

LiveShows watches the artists, bands and venues you pick and puts every upcoming Arizona show in one feed. It also hunts down every chance to win free tickets and music prizes, and texts you the moment one turns up.

Where it looks:

  • Instagram posts and reels from the accounts you follow, read for Arizona dates and giveaways
  • Ticketmaster Arizona dates for the artists you follow
  • JamBase every upcoming Arizona show, including small bands and club gigs, matched to your artists
  • Venue sites events pages you paste in, such as a bar's calendar
  • X giveaway posts from your artists, Arizona venues, Arizona radio stations and local promoters, searched twice a day
  • Contests JamBase Contests, SweepsAdvantage (Music, Tickets, Concerts) and SweepstakesBible (Tickets), checked every 4 hours and filtered to music prizes you can enter from Arizona

Shows drop off the feed the day after they happen, so it only ever shows what's coming up.

2. Sign in and put it on your home screen

  1. Go to liveshows.randombuilds.fyi.
  2. Sign in with your email and the temporary password from your welcome email.
  3. Pick your own password (8 or more characters). You stay signed in on that phone for 90 days.
  4. On iPhone: in Safari, tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen. LiveShows now opens like an app.

3. Follow artists, bands and venues

Tap Follow at the bottom. Pick a type, paste or type, tap Follow. The first check starts right away.

Instagram

Paste a handle (@bandname) or a profile link. LiveShows reads new posts and reels every 6 hours. Captions are read for Arizona show dates and for any "comment to win," "tag a friend" or ticket giveaway. Instagram Stories can't be read, so posts and reels only.

Artist

Type a band or artist name. This one name searches Ticketmaster, JamBase and X for that artist, so it's the best way to catch smaller bands. Tip: for a band you love, follow both their Instagram and their name.

Venue site

Paste a venue's events or calendar page, for example https://www.crescentphx.com/calendar. Every show on the page lands in your feed, and the page is checked every 6 hours. Some venue sites build their calendar in a way that can't be read. If that happens, the Follow tab tells you, and following those artists by name works instead.

Contest feeds

The contest directories are already on for you, listed at the bottom of the Follow tab as Contests. Tap pause on any you don't want.

Every source shows its status: green "Checked 2 hr ago" means it's working. If one says it couldn't be read, Jon gets an email automatically.

4. The Shows tab

  • Newest shows what was just announced at the top. By show date puts tonight first.
  • Each card shows the date, artist, venue, time and where it came from. "Tonight," "Tomorrow" or "In 3 days" shows up when it's close.
  • Tickets opens the ticket page. Original post or Venue page opens where the announcement came from.

5. The Giveaways tab

  • Every open contest for the things you follow, soonest deadline first. Red means it ends within 24 hours.
  • Each card says what you win, how to enter, and when it ends.
  • Enter opens the contest. Tap I entered afterward so you can see what's done. Hide removes one you don't want.
  • The red number on the tab is how many you haven't acted on yet.

LiveShows finds contests and tells you how to enter. You still enter each one yourself, since most need your own comment, follow or form.

6. Texts and the morning email

  • Texts: you get a text the moment LiveShows finds a giveaway from an artist, a venue, X, or an Arizona radio station, plus any directory contest for an Arizona show. Each text says the prize, the deadline, how to enter and the link. You get up to 15 a day, and anything past that is still in the app and the email. Reply STOP to turn texts off.
  • Morning email at 7:00 am: every new show and every new giveaway from the last day, including the nationwide music sweepstakes (vinyl, guitars, merch, trips) that don't get a text. No email on days when nothing is new.
  • Turn either one on or off under Me, Alerts.

7. Connect Apify, Ticketmaster and JamBase

These are free accounts in your name. Each gives you a key, which is a long code you copy once. Paste it in LiveShows under Me, Connections, then tap Save. LiveShows tests the key, stores it privately, and starts using it right away.

Apify (reads Instagram)

  1. Go to apify.com and tap Sign up. Signing up with Google works. Choose the Free plan. You don't need a card.
  2. Open console.apify.com, then Settings, then API & Integrations.
  3. Copy your Personal API token (it starts with apify_api_).
  4. In LiveShows: Me, Connections, Apify, paste, Save.

Cost: the free plan includes $5 of use every month. Instagram reading costs about $2.30 to $2.70 per 1,000 posts, and LiveShows only fetches new posts, so even 30 artists posting every day comes to about $2 to $3 a month.

Ticketmaster

  1. Go to developer.ticketmaster.com and register for a free account. Confirm the email they send.
  2. Sign in, open My Apps and click your default app.
  3. Copy the Consumer Key.
  4. In LiveShows: Me, Connections, Ticketmaster, paste, Save.

Free, with 5,000 searches a day. LiveShows uses a small fraction of that.

JamBase (small bands and club shows)

  1. Go to data.jambase.com and sign up. Pick the free Developer plan. You don't need a card.
  2. Open your account, then API keys, and copy your key (it starts with jbd_).
  3. In LiveShows: Me, Connections, JamBase, paste, Save.

The free plan allows 1,000 calls a month. LiveShows stops itself at 900, so it never runs into paid use. Connections shows how many have been used this month.

8. Bandsintown (optional)

Bandsintown blocks apps like this from reading it directly, so LiveShows can read it through your Apify account instead. Once a week it scans every Phoenix show listed on Bandsintown and matches them to your artists.

It costs about $4.50 a week ($15 per 1,000 shows), which is more than Apify's free $5 a month covers, so it starts off. To use it, add a payment method in Apify first, then turn on Bandsintown sweep under Me, Connections. JamBase already covers most small-band shows, so try JamBase first.

9. Evvntly, the extra free-ticket app

Evvntly gives away pairs of free tickets to concerts and comedy shows near you. Claiming happens inside its own app, so LiveShows can't see or claim them for you. It's worth having alongside LiveShows:

  1. Install Evvntly from the App Store or Google Play.
  2. Set your location to Phoenix and how far you'll travel.
  3. Turn on its notifications and claim tickets as they post. Inviting friends in Evvntly gets you extra entries.

10. Invites, passwords and help

  • Invite someone: Me, Invite someone. Enter their name and email, and they get a temporary password. Everyone follows their own artists and gets their own alerts.
  • Forgot your password: on the sign-in screen, type your email and tap "Forgot password?" You get a code that works for one hour.
  • Change your password: Me, Account.
  • Something looks wrong: reply to any LiveShows email and it goes to Jon. Jon also gets an automatic email whenever a source stops working.
